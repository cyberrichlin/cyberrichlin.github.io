YouTube Data Tool

YouTube Data Tool is a personal desktop application used solely by its owner to view analytics reports of his own YouTube channels. The app runs entirely on the owner's computer.

Privacy Policy

This app accesses YouTube Analytics reports, channel titles and the account email address in read-only mode only. All data and access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. Nothing is uploaded to any server, shared with third parties, or sold. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
